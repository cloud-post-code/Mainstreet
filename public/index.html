Our solution

Bringing Main Street online—connecting customers to artisans through local shops.

Each store connects its makers on one platform, where people can explore your combined assortment the way they would in person. We grow community by community, not seller by seller.

How it works

One platform ties together stores, artisans, and customers. AI handles listing, inventory, and much of the operational complexity.

shopping_bag When someone buys

  1. Shop Promote products on social media and in your newsletter.
  2. Customer Browse trusted local stores online and place orders.
  3. Shop & artisans Fulfill orders—makers and the shop can ship direct to buyers.

rocket_launch Getting your shop ready

  1. Shop Set up your shop page on the platform.
  2. Shop Onboard the artisans you already carry.
  3. Artisan List products for what sells in-store and what sells online.

What powers this

  • Guided setup & support — Straightforward navigation and help when you need it.
  • Autonomous AI assistant — Speeds up listings, updates, and repetitive shop work.
  • Mass AI listing & barcodeless scanning — Bring large catalogs online without living in spreadsheets.

What we focus on for your shop

Automation is making serious digital retail achievable for small teams—so you spend less time on tools and more time on the floor.

Key value propositions

  • 1
    Get you online simply AI-assisted storefronts, listings, and product imagery so you can launch a credible online presence without hiring a full ecommerce team.
  • 2
    Less work in operation Barcodeless scanning, bulk listing, and help keeping inventory and promotions current so your time stays on the floor, not in spreadsheets.
  • 3
    Expand beyond the limitations of physical retail Online checkout, pickup, local delivery, and your makers on the same storefront so sales are not capped by foot traffic and shelf space alone.

Why Main Street

Our advantage is community: we are built around local shops and the makers they carry—not around anonymous seller lists or a single merchant working alone.

Main Street

Crowded marketplaces

Everyone competes for the same search and feed. It is easy for a recognizable local identity to get lost next to thousands of unrelated sellers.

Solo storefront tools

Powerful for one brand, but shops that represent many makers often end up juggling listings, shipping, and marketing largely on their own.
